Tavern Optimize
Reuses prompt rows instead of rebuilding the list and moves token recounts out of the interaction path. Turning this off restores stock behaviour without a reload.
When off, token numbers show - until the recount finishes (stock behaviour).
On a phone the chat is repainted under every scroll, and each blurred bar, per-glyph halo and large shadow is rasterised again with it. This drops them and keeps transitions to transform and opacity. No effect on desktop, where the same effects are cheap.
Switching a preset writes about a hundred fields. Their handlers are replayed once at the end of the window, and a field the preset did not move keeps its handler idle.
If a replayed handler changes a field the preset just wrote, the field is listed in the report instead of being hidden.
Stores token counts per model and content, so switching back to a preset is instant even after a restart.
